Lady Luck Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 5, 2026

Introduction

Lady Luck is a Discord bot designed to provide conversational interactions, entertainment, and community engagement within Discord servers.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Lady Luck processes, how it is used, and the choices available to users.

Information We Process

When you interact with Lady Luck, we may process:

Discord User ID
Discord Server ID
Discord Channel ID
Messages intentionally sent to Lady Luck or messages containing supported trigger phrases
Basic operational metadata necessary for providing the service

Lady Luck does not collect passwords, payment information, or personal information outside of what Discord provides through its API.

How Information Is Used

Information is processed solely to:

Detect supported commands and trigger phrases
Generate responses
Maintain limited conversational context where applicable
Prevent abuse and spam
Diagnose bugs and improve reliability
AI Processing

Some interactions may be securely transmitted to OpenAI's API for the purpose of generating conversational responses.

Only the information necessary to generate a response is transmitted.

Data Retention

Operational logs and temporary conversation context may be retained for debugging, abuse prevention, and service reliability.

Data is not sold or used for advertising.

Third Parties

Lady Luck may use trusted service providers including:

Discord
OpenAI
Railway (hosting)

These providers may process information necessary for operation of the service.

Server Administrator Control

Server administrators control:

Which servers Lady Luck joins
Which channels Lady Luck can access
The permissions granted to Lady Luck

Removing the bot immediately ends future access to that server.

Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically.

Continued use of Lady Luck after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
