Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Lady Luck processes, how that information is used, and the choices available to users.

Information we process

When users interact with Lady Luck, the bot may process Discord user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, message content submitted through supported commands or trigger phrases, and limited operational metadata necessary to provide the service.

Lady Luck does not collect Discord passwords, payment-card information, or private information outside the data made available through Discord's API and user interactions.

How information is used

  • Detect supported commands and trigger phrases.
  • Generate and deliver responses.
  • Maintain limited conversational context where applicable.
  • Prevent abuse, spam, and service disruption.
  • Diagnose bugs and maintain service reliability.

AI processing

Some conversational interactions may be transmitted to OpenAI's API for the purpose of generating a response. Only information necessary to provide the requested interaction is transmitted.

Data retention

Lady Luck may temporarily retain limited conversational context and operational logs for functionality, debugging, security, and abuse prevention. Information is not sold or used for targeted advertising.

Third-party services

Lady Luck may rely on service providers such as Discord, OpenAI, and Railway. These providers may process information as necessary to operate their respective services.

Server administrator control

Server administrators control the servers and channels Lady Luck can access through Discord's permission system. Removing the bot or revoking its permissions prevents future access.
